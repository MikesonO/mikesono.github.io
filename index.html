About Me

I'm a Frontend Developer with 4+ years of professional experience, currently at Incisive Media where my role covers a lot of ground. Day to day that means building and maintaining web pages, integrating APIs, setting up analytics via GTM and GA4, and developing backend services using Node.js and Azure Functions.

It's a varied role without a lot of overlap with colleagues, which means I've gotten pretty good at figuring things out independently, coordinating with third-party suppliers, and finding pragmatic solutions under pressure.

I care about clean, well-structured code and I'm usually working on something outside of work too. Right now that's mostly React and getting more comfortable with full-stack development.
